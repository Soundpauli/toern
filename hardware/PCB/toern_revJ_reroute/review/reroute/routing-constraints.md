## Stackup and fabrication

The saved board is nominally 1.60 mm with 35 µm copper (about 1 oz) on all four layers, 0.10 mm outer FR-4 prepregs and a 1.24 mm core, all with entered εr 4.5 and loss tangent 0.02. Outer solder mask is entered as 0.01 mm. F.Cu carries short pin escapes, component loops and signals over In1.Cu. B.Cu carries long crossings and selected power trunks over In2.Cu. Reserve both inner layers for continuous GND through signal corridors, with nearby GND stitching at signal transitions and through the connector return regions. Keep top and bottom GND infill where it stays connected. Preserve uninterrupted return copper around the Teensy and both USB routes. Charger and amp return branches should close locally into the common plane, away from codec returns.

The project has one 0.10 mm-clearance default net class, 0.20 mm preferred signal width and 0.50/0.30 mm via geometry. Keep its 0.50 mm copper-to-edge, 0.25 mm hole-to-hole and hole-to-copper settings, minimum 0.10 mm via ring, and two resolved thermal spokes. Existing GND pour uses 0.30 mm clearance, 0.25 mm minimum copper, and 0.50 mm thermal gap and spoke defaults. Use 0.15–0.20 mm for ordinary signals and 0.25–0.35 mm for quiet local supply links, with clearance ordinarily 0.15–0.20 mm and 0.10 mm at constrained fanout when necessary. Use 0.60/0.30 mm through vias where space permits, 0.50/0.30 mm in dense escapes and parallel vias for power and thermal returns. The fab's profile dated 2026-11-18 gives 0.09 mm base multilayer track and spacing and 0.45/0.20 mm base via geometry. [Fab capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities)

## Power widths and budgets

- J4 VBUS feeds charger IN and EN2, with EN1 grounded. Under valid VBUS the 1.1 kΩ ILIM programs about 1.46 A typical (1.36–1.56 A from factor tolerance before resistor tolerance and device limits). The 590 Ω ISET requests about 1.51 A typical fast charge, subject to available input, charge state and thermal regulation. JP1 can feed S1 from VBUS or VYS. Treat the long VBUS, VYS, VBAT and main +5V routes as high-current copper. Use 1.0 mm minimum on shorter 1.5 A trunks where practical, widening long VBUS/+5V spans to 1.5–2.0 mm or paired copper pours/tracks, with 0.3–0.5 mm short necks where pad geometry requires. At 20 °C, 35 µm copper is approximately 0.49 mΩ/square. A 160 mm × 1.5 mm run drops about 79 mV at 1.5 A before via, return, contact and hot-copper losses. Use roughly 0.8–1.0 mm for U8 load output and other amp/load feed trunks where space permits. Confirm actual current budgets, connector/source capability and thermal rise in hardware. The schematic does not detect the source's advertised USB-C current. [TI BQ24075 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24075.pdf)
- J33 VBUS is fed solely from CN3 on 5V-HOST, with the return through CN6/CN7 and carrier GND. Use about 0.8–1.0 mm for the long host trunk, 0.25–0.4 mm short connector fanouts, and paired vias where changing layers. At 500 mA, 100 mm × 0.8 mm alone drops about 31 mV, excluding Teensy switch, pogo, via, hot-copper and return losses. Use 500 mA as a carrier routing budget, subject to upstream supply, Teensy switch and contact capability. R11 and R33 remain independent 56 kΩ pulls from J33 CC1/A5 and CC2/B5 to 5V-HOST. [PJRC Teensy 4.1](https://www.pjrc.com/store/teensy41.html) · [PJRC pinout](https://www.pjrc.com/teensy/card11b_rev4_web.pdf)
- SPX3819's 500 mA rating is conditional on dissipation and dropout. At 5 V input, a 3.3 V SOT-23 regulator at 500 mA would dissipate 0.85 W, corresponding to about 162 °C rise at the datasheet's 191 °C/W figure. U2's 1.8 V rail serves U3 VDDD. ## USB geometry and timing

Device J4 A6/B6 → CN2 and A7/B7 → CN1. Host J33 A6/B6 → CN5 and A7/B7 → CN4. Both are high-speed-capable. Prefer a single outer layer per trunk, with continuous adjacent GND and matched geometry and via count. A first-pass edge-coupled microstrip estimate using saved h = 0.10 mm, t = 0.035 mm and εr = 4.5 gives about 90 Ω at 0.13 mm trace and 0.13 mm edge gap. The calculation uses Z0 = (87/√(εr+1.41)) ln(5.98h/(0.8w+t)) and Zdiff ≈ 2Z0(1−0.48e^(−0.96s/h)). The fab must verify finished geometry, solder mask, actual laminate and impedance. Keep unrelated copper at least 0.30 mm from the pair where possible. Match both receptacle orientations, including A/B branch lengths and electrical layer traversal. Aim for ≤0.5 mm PCB centerline skew per orientation and use symmetric transitions or local compensation at the source of the mismatch. Long detours would worsen this fixed geometry. Connector-to-pogo straight-line spans alone are roughly 80 mm device and 90–94 mm host, beyond TI's generic 75 mm high-speed example. The baseline copper totals are longer and include branches. Physical channel performance requires measurement with the mated connector and pogo interface. ## I²S timing

The checked-in firmware uses Teensy AudioInputI2S and AudioOutputI2S (platformio/audioinit.h). With the Teensy Audio configuration, LRCLK is nominally 44.1 kHz, MCLK 11.2896 MHz and BCLK 2.8224 MHz for 64 clocks/frame (1.4112 MHz for 32). At 64 clocks/frame, the opposite-edge interval is about 177 ns. NXP lists 10 ns setup, 10 ns hold, and up to 10 ns output delay. Use direct referenced traces and target under 1 ns incremental routing skew between BCLK and data, where feasible. Baseline track totals across both resistor sides are about 29 mm MCLK, 40 mm BCLK, 35 mm LRCLK, 62 mm DIN and 60 mm DOUT, giving around 0.2 ns difference at 6 ps/mm between longest and shortest before counting vias. Trace impedance and ringback are still relevant to edge rate. R1/R2/R3/R5 sit 20–33 mm from their Teensy source pins, which limits their effectiveness as source terminators at fixed placement. R4 is 3.65 mm from the codec DOUT driver. Preserve all five 22 Ω values and route both sides directly.
