Sample voices 1–8

One lane per channel. Bitcrush is before the state-variable filter. Voices 3 and 4 have no reverb object — they jump from the filter mixer into mixer1.

Channel 11 — 3-voice poly

Three oscillators × three voices, pan-split into L/R mixers, then collapsed to mono for the shared bitcrush / filter / reverb chain. Leftover waveform11_1/2 objects still exist but are muted (gain 0) so they cannot leak into reverb while stopped.

Channels 13–14 — mono synths

No reverb (filter page 0 uses CENT in that slot). Two oscillators into a mixer, then a single envelope as VCA.

Worst-case samples: 3 hot hits on a bank → mixer bus ≈ 1.0; both banks into mixer_end at 0.50 + 0.50 = 1.0 before synth (0.60) and monitor. Four simultaneous full-level hits on one bank can clip — drop MIX_BUS_HEADROOM to 0.25 if that is a real pattern.

Stereo out and codec

VOL → 2-CH writes mixer_stereoL/R. Hardware: R is I2S DAC 0, L is DAC 1.

L+R taps mixer1 (voices 1–4) to left and mixer2 (5–8) to right, and mutes mixer_end on both sides — synths 11/13/14 and input monitor drop out of that mode. Stereo in 3 is always 0.

What is not in the graph

  • Rows 9, 10, 12 — no voice objects.
  • Channels 3, 4, 13, 14 — no freeverb.
  • envelope11 — patched into mixer_waveform11 in2, but that input is held at 0. Poly envelopes are Senvelope1/2.
  • USB audio is not enabled. USB is Serial + MIDI only.
Clipping checklist: four sample hits on one bank; ch11 RVRB stacked on dry at 0.20 + 0.20; BITC + RVRB (crush before the tanks). Channel mute releases envelopes so tails do not sit in the pool.
