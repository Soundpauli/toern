TOERN REV H — JLCPCB manufacturing package

Upload these three files from production_files/:
  GERBER-toern_revH.zip  — PCB fabrication, 4 copper layers, mask, silk,
                          paste, edge cuts and separate PTH/NPTH drills
  BOM-toern_revH.csv     — JLCPCB assembly parts
  CPL-toern_revH.csv     — matching placement data, millimetres, degrees

Order a 4-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4 board, top-side assembly. Copper order is
F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu. The outline is approximately 160 x 71 mm.
Select surface finish, colours and quantity in the quote as desired.

Assembly scope follows hardware/PCB/BOM/README.md: include through-hole
jacks, JST headers, encoder sockets, switch, microphone and CN1/CN2 pogo
pins. Arrange the necessary through-hole assembly service in the quote.
J28-J31 are hand-soldered 3M Teensy sockets and are excluded from both
upload CSVs. U6 (Teensy), mounting holes, logos and JP1 are also excluded.

D2 is BAT54S, C171786, SOT-23:
  pin 1 = GND (anode), pin 2 = +3.3 V (cathode), pin 3 = clock clamp node.
The exported copper, not just the BOM label, is checked for this correction.

References with descriptive labels in KiCad are renamed in the manufacturing
snapshot only, to keep BOM/CPL designators simple and identical to Gerber
attributes and the assembly drawing. See review/reference-map.csv:
  source J11-5V -> J11; source J11-3.3V -> J32
  source J12 - BUTTON_A-C -> J12
  source J13 - TOUCHYTOUCH -> J13
  source J14 + BUTTON_C -> J14
All six 5-pin JST headers are included (J11-J15 and J32).

Parts data:
  R24 is 590R, C166635 (RALEC RTT02591JTH), 0402, +/-5%, as selected by
  the user. PCB, schematic, BOM and placement data use this same selection.
  This programs approximately 1.5 A nominal fast-charge current; actual
  current is limited by the input supply, system load and thermal regulation.
  C34 uses the source PCB selection C15195, 10nF, 0402. The previous
  export's C57112 is a 0603 part and does not match the PCB footprint.
  C171786 is listed as BAT54S, SOT-23 by JLCPCB.
  Catalog identities checked 2026-11-09 using JLCPCB's component-detail API:
  https://jlcpcb.com/api/overseas-pcb-order/v1/smtComponentShopCart/getComponentDetail?componentCode=C166635
  (replace componentCode for the other part numbers).

Placement coordinates use the centre of the pad bounding box, relative
to the board's drill/place origin, Y positive upwards. The installed JLCPCB
plugin's rotation corrections are preserved: 180 degrees for Conn_01x02,
Conn_01x03 and Microphone_Condenser; 270 degrees for PAM8403D.
Verify pin-1/polarity against review/assembly-top.svg in JLCPCB's placement
preview, particularly D2, IC1, U3, U4, U8, C6 and the connectors. These
local exports have not been uploaded to JLCPCB or approved by its engineers.
JLCPCB CPL reference: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pick-place-file-for-pcb-assembly

The DRC report has no copper/clearance errors or unconnected items at
generation. Existing library/silkscreen warnings and schematic parity
issues (Teensy pads and connector reference names) remain documented in
DRC.json. This is an export validation, not a complete electrical redesign
review. Resolve component stock, sourcing and placement in the JLCPCB quote.

Use only the files packaged together here. Old Gerber/ and other archived
exports elsewhere in the repository are historical, not this release.
